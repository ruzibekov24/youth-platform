# Assumptions

Bu yerda yozilganlarning barchasi research orqali tekshirilmaguncha gipoteza.

## Product assumptions

1. Yoshlar opportunity, learning, people va projectlar orasida fragmentationni boshdan kechiradi.
2. Bog‘langan tajriba alohida directory'dan foydaliroq bo‘lishi mumkin.
3. User real actionga yordam olsa qaytadi.
4. Relevance va personalization opportunity overloadni kamaytirishi mumkin.
5. Young builderlar uchun team topish muammo bo‘lishi mumkin.
6. Userlar real ish evidence'ini qadrlashi mumkin.
7. Idea-to-project layer passive discoveryni actionga aylantirishi mumkin.
8. Youth product lokal boshlanib, keyin kengayishi mumkin.

## Riskli taxminlar

### “Hammaga kerak.”
Noto‘g‘ri bo‘lishi mumkin. Birinchi segmentni topish kerak.

### “Userlar content yaratadi.”
Noma’lum. Contribution incentive'larini tushunish kerak.

### “Userlar project quradi.”
Noma’lum. Challenge, prompt yoki partnership kerak bo‘lishi mumkin.

### “AI discoveryni hal qiladi.”
Avtomatik emas. Yomon recommendation ko‘proq noise beradi.

### “Katta platforma mavjud tool'larni yengadi.”
Shart emas. Aniq connected workflow kerak.

## Validation methods

- interview;
- survey;
- usability test;
- landing-page experiment;
- manual opportunity curation;
- community pilot;
- project challenge;
- concierge MVP.

Birinchi MVP software emas, service yoki community process bo‘lishi ham mumkin.
