# Open Questions

These must be answered before serious implementation.

## User
1. Who is the first user segment?
2. What is their most painful recurring problem?
3. What do they currently use?
4. What makes them return?
5. What would they trust us with?

## Opportunity discovery
6. Which categories matter most?
7. How should opportunities be verified?
8. How should eligibility be represented?
9. How should recommendations work?
10. Who maintains opportunity data?

## MIYA
11. Do users actually want an idea-to-project workflow?
12. What is the smallest useful version?
13. How do we prevent idea graveyards?
14. How do we help projects move from concept to action?

## People
15. What makes teammate matching useful?
16. Should matching use skills, goals, availability, location, or all?
17. How do we handle trust and safety?
18. How do mentors participate?

## Identity
19. What counts as meaningful evidence?
20. How are achievements verified?
21. Should profiles be public by default?
22. How do we protect younger users?

## Sustainability
23. Who pays, if anyone?
24. Could organizations sponsor challenges or programs?
25. How can the platform remain useful without becoming advertising-first?

## Geography
26. Should the first market be Uzbekistan?
27. What must be localized?
28. What changes when expanding internationally?

## Technology
29. What should we build ourselves?
30. What should integrate with existing platforms?
31. Where is AI actually useful?
32. What is the minimum technical architecture?

## Most important unresolved question

> **What is the single problem that would make a young person say: "I need this"?**
