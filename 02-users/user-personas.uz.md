# User Personas

Bular real odamlar emas, research uchun boshlang‘ich tasavvurlar.

## Persona 1 — Ambitious student

**Yosh:** 15–17

Study abroad, programlar, projectlar va skill developmentni xohlaydi.

Fikri:
> Nimadir katta qilishni xohlayman, lekin internetda juda ko‘p narsa bor va nimasi foydali ekanini bilmayman.

Needs: relevant opportunities, next steps, learning, peers, projects, portfolio.

Frustrations: tarqoq information, deadline, eligibility noaniqligi, sifatsiz postlar.

## Persona 2 — Young builder

**Yosh:** 16–22

Technical, creative, business yoki organizational skillga ega.

Fikri:
> Nimadir qura olaman, lekin to‘g‘ri muammo va to‘g‘ri odamlarni topish qiyin.

Needs: problems, teammates, project structure, feedback, mentors, visibility.

## Persona 3 — Contributor

**Yosh:** 16–24

Event, volunteering, mentoring yoki communityga hissa qo‘shishni xohlaydi.

Fikri:
> Menda boshqalarga beradigan narsa bor, faqat to‘g‘ri joy kerak.

Needs: contribution opportunities, trusted organizations, events, recognition, network.

## Research eslatmasi

Real interview va behavior bu personlarni o‘zgartirishi kerak. Biz taxminlarga yopishib qolmaymiz.
