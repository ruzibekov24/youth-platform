# User Personas

These are fictional research tools, not descriptions of real individuals.

## Persona 1 — The ambitious student

**Age:** 15–17

Wants to study, join programs, build projects, and improve skills.

Typical thought:
> I want to do something impressive, but there are so many things online that I do not know what is actually worth doing.

Needs: relevant opportunities, clear next steps, learning paths, peers, projects, portfolio.

Frustrations: scattered information, deadlines, eligibility uncertainty, low-quality posts, difficulty finding peers.

## Persona 2 — The young builder

**Age:** 16–22

Has technical, creative, business, or organizational skills.

Typical thought:
> I can build something, but finding the right problem and the right people is harder.

Needs: problems, teammates, project structure, feedback, mentors, visibility.

## Persona 3 — The contributor

**Age:** 16–24

Wants to organize events, volunteer, mentor, or contribute.

Typical thought:
> I have something to offer. I just need the right place to contribute.

Needs: contribution opportunities, trusted organizations, events, recognition, network.

## Research warning

Real interviews and behavior should change these personas if the evidence disagrees with them.
