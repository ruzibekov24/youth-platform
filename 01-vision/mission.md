# Mission & Principles

## Working mission

Build infrastructure that helps young people discover, connect, create, and grow.

## Principles

### 1. Action over consumption
The platform should not optimize for endless scrolling.

### 2. Relevance over volume
Useful opportunities matter more than huge lists.

### 3. People before vanity metrics
Profiles should help people understand and collaborate.

### 4. Ideas need execution
An idea is only the beginning. The system should help create concrete next steps.

### 5. Youth agency
Users should be able to explore options and build their own paths.

### 6. Trust matters
Opportunity information and achievements should eventually have clear provenance and verification.

### 7. Build gradually
A huge vision does not require a huge first release.

### 8. Local first, global by design
The first version may be especially useful for Uzbekistan while avoiding unnecessary geographic limitations.

### 9. AI is a tool, not the product
AI should reduce friction and improve discovery or execution, not exist merely because it is fashionable.
