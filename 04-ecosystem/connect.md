# Connect

## 🇺🇿 O‘zbekcha

Connect follower yig‘ish emas, **mazmunli odamlar bilan bog‘lanish** haqida. Bu yerda do‘stlar, team, mentorlar, organizerlar, volunteerlar, communities va organizations uchrashishi mumkin.

## 🇬🇧 English

Connect is about meaningful relationships, not collecting followers. Possible connections include peers, teammates, mentors, organizers, volunteers, communities, and organizations.

## 🇷🇺 Русский

Connect — это не про количество подписчиков, а про **значимые связи**: друзей, команды, наставников, организаторов, волонтёров, сообществ и организаций.
