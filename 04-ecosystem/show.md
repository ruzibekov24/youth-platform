# Identity & Show

## 🇺🇿 O‘zbekcha

Profile oddiy bio emas. U yoshning **rivojlanish yo‘li**ni ko‘rsatadigan living record bo‘lishi kerak. Projectlar, contribution, real work orqali ko‘rsatilgan skilllar, eventlar, volunteering, leadership, publications, verified achievements va community impact bo‘lishi mumkin.

> **Haqiqiy ishni ko‘rinadigan qilish.**

## 🇬🇧 English

The profile should not be a static bio. It should become a **living record of the person's journey**: projects, contributions, skills demonstrated through work, events, volunteering, leadership, publications, verified achievements, and community impact.

> **Make real work visible.**

## 🇷🇺 Русский

Профиль не должен быть просто биографией. Это **живой след пути человека**: проекты, вклад, навыки, мероприятия, волонтёрство, лидерство, публикации, достижения и влияние на сообщество.
