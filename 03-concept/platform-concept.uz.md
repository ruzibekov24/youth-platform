# Platform Concept

## Ishchi concept

Quyidagilarni birlashtiradigan youth ecosystem:

**Opportunities + Learning + Ideas + People + Projects + Achievements**

Muhim narsa — obyektlarning o‘zi emas, ularning o‘zaro bog‘lanishi.

## Core objects

**Person** — identity, interests, skills, goals, contributions va evidence.

**Opportunity** — scholarship, competition, event, volunteering, internship, program, grant va boshqa imkoniyat.

**Problem** — tushunish yoki hal qilishga arziydigan real muammo.

**Idea** — muammoga taklif qilinayotgan yechim.

**Team** — umumiy maqsad uchun ishlaydigan odamlar.

**Project** — goal, odamlar, tasklar, progress va outcome'ga ega real harakat.

**Resource** — learning material, guide, mentor, tool yoki boshqa support.

**Achievement** — participation, completion, contribution, skill yoki impact evidence'i.

## Asosiy relationship

**Opportunity topish → o‘rganish → odamlar topish → idea yaratish → team tuzish → build → achievement → keyingi opportunity.**

## Social network emas

Platforma asosan:
- generic feed;
- follower competition;
- repost;
- engagement uchun engagement

bo‘lib qolmasligi kerak.

Social feature faqat learning, collaboration yoki actionga xizmat qilsa kerak.

## Faqat job board ham emas

Education, competition, volunteering, entrepreneurship, research, leadership, creativity, technology, community projects va career imkoniyatlari ham kirishi mumkin.
